Recurring bookkeeping

Establishing a recurring schedule enables automatic bookkeeping on billing dates unless you select manual recording. Automatically recorded entries are unverified until you confirm the payment. Scheduled processing can be delayed or fail. Pause or cancel a schedule when you no longer want future entries. Disconnecting an assistant or revoking its token does not stop standing schedules, delete records or cancel a merchant charge.
